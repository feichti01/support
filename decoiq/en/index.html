Status

DecoIQ is in development and not yet on the App Store. Planned for the first version:

  • Open circuit and CCR with bailout: profile, gases and runtime with gradient factors; bailout and “what if” at every point.
  • Change any parameter without starting over; compare GF sets.
  • Gas rules such as the rule of thirds, gas switches on every page.
  • Repetitive dives with surface interval and residual gas; check whether a longer stay still works out.
  • Plan from wreck and cave sketches; look up background on the web if you wish.
  • Runtimes in familiar formats; plans on all devices, hand-over to BlendIQ and DiveIQ.

DecoIQ is a planning tool. It does not replace training or a dive computer; your dive computer, training and team remain authoritative.
