Status

DiveIQ is in development and not yet on the App Store. Planned for the first version:

  • Move over from Diving Log: your whole logbook with dives, sites, buddies and certifications; plus UDDF and other logbooks.
  • Read dive computers via Bluetooth; take over many dives at once from a Shearwater Cloud export.
  • Import Apple Watch Ultra dives from Apple Health.
  • Dive sites with map and sketch, trips, dive centres, equipment and reminders.
  • Analysis of how you dived, statistics, no-fly time and desaturation.
  • Open dives in BlendIQ with one tap; export as UDDF, CSV and PDF.
  • Works offline; your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud.

DiveIQ is a logbook and analysis tool. It does not replace training or a dive computer.
