This policy applies to GasBlender on macOS, iPhone, and iPad, to beta versions distributed through Apple TestFlight, and to the public support site at feichti01.github.io/support. Apple’s and GitHub’s own services are also governed by their respective privacy policies.

3. Processing within the app

GasBlender does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The app has no user accounts and uses no advertising, tracking technology, analytics SDKs, cloud synchronization, or developer-operated server.

Planning inputs, prices, gas and cylinder presets, and app settings are stored only on the device. The developer cannot access this local data. You can remove it through the app’s features or by deleting the app and its local data.

Apple handles app downloads, transactions, and distribution as an independent controller under the Apple Privacy Policy.

When TestFlight is used, Apple automatically collects beta testing data, including crash logs and usage information, and may provide it to the app provider together with your name and email address. Voluntary TestFlight feedback can contain text, screenshots, and other personal information. The developer uses beta testing data and feedback supplied through TestFlight only to diagnose errors and improve GasBlender. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional app under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. See Apple TestFlight & Privacy for details.

5. Support site and GitHub

The operator does not place first-party cookies or use first-party analytics or advertising on this static website. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors to GitHub Pages for security purposes. Details are provided in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Public support requests are processed as GitHub Issues. Their contents, user name, and any other information you submit are publicly visible. Do not post confidential information. Use the email address above for private privacy requests.

6. Contact

If you contact the developer by email, the information you provide is processed to handle and answer your request. Depending on the request, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR or the legitimate interest in support and secure operation under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Statutory retention obligations remain unaffected.

7. Recipients, international transfers, and retention

Apple is used for app distribution and beta testing, while GitHub is used for hosting and public issues. Both providers may process data outside the European Economic Area and describe recipients, legal bases, and safeguards for international transfers in their privacy statements.

The developer retains contact, feedback, and diagnostic data only as long as needed to respond, diagnose errors, and improve the app, or as required by law. Public GitHub Issues remain visible in the project and GitHub history unless deleted or anonymized. Local app data remains on the device until changed or deleted by the user.

Subject to the GDPR, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Consent can be withdrawn at any time with future effect. Contact the controller by email to exercise your rights.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Austria, contact the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, email: dsb@dsb.gv.at.
